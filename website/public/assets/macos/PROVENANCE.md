# Apple menu glyph

`apple-logo.png` is the installed system symbol `apple.logo`, rasterized locally. The site uses it as a mask for the menu-bar and download-button glyph. Apple retains its rights in it.

The pictures of the app in `../app` are screenshots of Notchy's own interface, rendered from its SwiftUI views with made-up data (see `src/lib/shots.js`). They show what Notchy draws, including the system app icons in the Home tab's app grid and the Spotify badge on its music card; those marks belong to their owners. The landscape images in `../img` are original generated artwork.
