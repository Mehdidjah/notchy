# Audience card icons

These six SVGs are the unmodified regular-weight icons from @phosphor-icons/core 2.1.1, by Phosphor Icons, under the included MIT license.

Source: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/phosphor-core
Package: https://registry.npmjs.org/@phosphor-icons/core/-/core-2.1.1.tgz

- Designers: paint-brush
- Developers: code-block
- Creators: pencil-line
- Busy days: calendar-check
- Multitaskers: browsers
- Your everyday Mac: laptop

They are served locally without a new JavaScript dependency or icon CDN. The 24px glyphs sit inside 64px white badges with a 30px corner radius, matching the reference's icon treatment. The adjacent headings provide the text meaning, so the glyphs are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
