First time opening Notchy?

Notchy isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. 1

    Open Notchy from Applications. When macOS says it can't verify it, click Done.

  2. 2

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down.

  3. 3

    Click Open Anyway, enter your password, then click Open Anyway again.

You'll do this again after each update, and Notchy may ask for its permissions again.
